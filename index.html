Sorting Shift

Prototype · v0.3

You run the sorting line at a recycling centre. Drag each item off the belt into the right bin. Sort it right and the bale stays clean. Sort it wrong and you contaminate the load.

  • Drag the item to a bin, tap a bin, or press its number key. No timer.
  • Every answer comes with a one-line reason, right or wrong.
  • Items marked varies locally are sorted differently in some towns. More than one bin may count as right.
  • Items you get wrong come back sooner next shift.
